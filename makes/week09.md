## Process Notes
How did you make this?

- It took me a few tries to get what I was looking for. I started with a style and 2-3 artists that I wanted to take inspiration from. For example, Beyoncé, Rihanna, and Mariah Carey. This created the instrumentals behind the lyrics. Then I found that this program didn’t automatically give you lyrics to the song. From here, I used ChatGPT to generate lyrics with the same prompt that was given to Suno. I then inserted the lyrics, and it generated a song based on everything it was given. After my first few generations, I took the same prompt and changed certain words so they could fit a different style of music. 

What parameters did you control?

- I controlled almost everything on this website leading up to the outcome. I was able to control the style of the song, which was a super cool thing to experiment with. I was able to input my own lyrics, which, if I was gifted in that section, I would create my lyrics. However, I made the choice to have ChatGPT generate lyrics for me. From there, I was able to see if the lyrics were on theme, which they usually were. As you can see, my choices were very purposeful, and I tried to be in as much control as possible in order to get the outcomes I wanted. 

How many total outputs did you generate?

- I generated around 8 songs; however, my first 2 did not have any lyrics, which is why I did not include them. After inputting lyrics, I experimented with different styles. I chose to include a range of different styles like hip-hop, R&B, Pop, and Emotional music. Experimenting with all different styles helped me generate more outputs, which allowed me to fine-tune the outputs into something that was extremely beneficial to me. From there, my selection came from whatever I thought sounded best.

## Reflection
I think creativity is something that is considered “out of the box” when it comes to ideas for something. I think that AI can be creative because it has become a social construct that humans are starting to use. Because of this, AI knows what it means to be creative. As a result of this, we must work towards something that is inherently human in the process of creativity. The main thing I can think of is the emotional and historical connection. This could be the motivation behind why you chose a certain topic or why you chose a certain way to express the topic. We can use these feelings as ways of differentiating us from AI. AI does not have reasons for the why; we tell it what to do, and it cannot give us that motivation because it doesn’t have any. In my process, I think humans drive it forward. Without us being in charge, this process would not have started. I created the ideas for the song, and that influenced everything that I did from then on. I would consider the human an administrator who is overseeing the process and making sure that it is coming along as it was intended to.
